Standard Operating Procedure

4-Stage Smartphone Capture Protocol

Indoor photogrammetry requires strict spatial parallax rather than casual video recording. Our protocol ensures robust Structure-from-Motion registration on everyday mobile devices.

PHASE 01

Room Conditioning

Clear floor pathways, turn off oscillating fans, stabilize diffuse indoor lighting, and place high-contrast anchor patterns on bare, featureless walls.

PHASE 02

Camera Configuration

Standard 1.0× wide lens in 1080p @ 30 fps landscape mode. Mandatory AE/AF lock freezes exposure and focus, preserving photometric consistency across views.

PHASE 03

Dual-Loop Trajectory

Execute a 45–75 second heel-to-toe "ninja walk" (0.5–0.8 m/s). Outer perimeter pass followed by an inward-facing interior orbit around central furniture.

PHASE 04

Stationary Buffers

Hold completely still for 3–4 seconds at the start and end of recording to absorb screen-tap vibration within the automated 5% trim filter.
